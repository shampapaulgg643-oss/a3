Who we are

Making the sounds of English easier to learn

Phonetics Halo is a small, independent education project sharing clear and free pronunciation resources for learners and teachers around the world.

Our story

Born from a very common question

“Why does English spelling not match the way people talk?” We heard that question again and again from students, colleagues and friends learning English as an additional language. Most pronunciation materials we found were either too academic or too shallow to help.

So we started writing our own explanations: short, practical and grounded in real phonetics. Phonetics Halo is the home for those lessons. The “halo” is the ring of sound around every word, the part that spelling alone never shows.

Clarity over jargon

We use technical terms only when they help, and we always explain them in plain language.

Every accent is valid

We teach for clear communication. Your accent is part of who you are; we just help you be understood.

Free and open

All lessons and exercises are free. We rely on careful writing, not paywalls or courses.

How we create lessons

Grounded in phonetics, tested with learners

Our explanations follow standard descriptions of English phonetics used in linguistics and language teaching. Before publishing, we try each exercise with real learners of different first languages and adjust anything that causes confusion.

Examples use General American English, and we note important differences in other accents when they matter. Read our Editorial Policy for the full process.

What we are not

A friendly guide, not a clinic

Phonetics Halo is an educational resource about pronunciation and speech sounds. We are not speech therapists and we do not diagnose or treat speech, language or hearing conditions. If you have concerns in those areas, please speak with a qualified professional.
