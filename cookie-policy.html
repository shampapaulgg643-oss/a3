Cookie Policy

Which cookies Phonetics Halo uses and how you can manage them.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Phonetics Halo uses cookies and similar technologies on www.phoneticshalo.com. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how pages are used. First-party cookies are set by us; third-party cookies are set by services we use, such as analytics or advertising providers.

Cookies we use

Essential

These remember basic choices, such as whether you accepted our cookie notice, using your browser’s local storage. They are needed for the site to work properly and do not track you across other websites.

Analytics

Google Analytics cookies, such as _ga and _ga_*, help us understand how many people visit, which lessons they use and how they navigate. They typically last up to two years and give us aggregated, not personal, insights.

Advertising

If ads are shown, Google and other vendors may use cookies to display ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

Use our cookie notice to accept all cookies or keep to essential ones. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Updates

We may update this policy when our tools or legal requirements change. The date above shows the latest version.
