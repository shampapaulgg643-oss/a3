Not professional or clinical advice

Our lessons explain pronunciation and phonetics for language learning. They are not speech therapy and must not be used to diagnose or treat any speech, language, voice or hearing condition. If you or someone you care for has concerns about speech development or communication, please consult a qualified speech-language pathologist or medical professional.

Accent and variation

English is spoken in many accents, and there is no single correct pronunciation. Our examples are based mainly on General American English. Your own accent, or the accent you are learning, may differ in ways that are completely valid.

Audio examples

Listen buttons use your browser’s built-in text-to-speech voice. Quality and accent vary by device and browser, and synthetic voices may not always pronounce sounds exactly as described. Use them as a helpful guide alongside recordings of real speakers.
