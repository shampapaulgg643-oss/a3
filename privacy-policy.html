Privacy Policy

How Phonetics Halo collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Phonetics Halo (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates www.phoneticshalo.com, a free educational website about English pronunciation. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you provide

If you contact us, we receive your name, email address, chosen topic and message. If you subscribe to Sound of the Week, we receive your email address. We use this information only to answer you or send the newsletter you requested. We do not ask for payment or sensitive personal details.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, we automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time spent on the site. This is mostly collected through cookies, described in our Cookie Policy.

Audio features

The listen buttons on our site use your browser’s built-in speech synthesis to read example words aloud. This happens on your own device. We do not record your voice or collect any audio from you.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages and questions.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which lessons are useful and improve the website.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how visitors use our website. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and session length, which Google processes under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display ads provided by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers who help run the site, such as hosting and email delivery, and only as needed. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our users and website.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

Depending on your location, including under the GDPR or CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request, and unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link provided.

Children

Our content is suitable for learners of many ages, but the website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us so we can delete it. Parents and teachers are welcome to use our lessons with younger students.

Security

We use reasonable measures, including HTTPS encryption and limited access, to protect information. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.
